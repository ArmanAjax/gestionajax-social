Managing a condo on the South Shore

The Longueuil agglomeration brings together Longueuil (including Vieux-Longueuil, Saint-Hubert and Greenfield Park), Brossard, Boucherville, Saint-Bruno-de-Montarville and Saint-Lambert. Since July 2023, Brossard has been served by three REM stations — Brossard, Du Quartier and Panama. From a small building in Boucherville to a large complex in Brossard, every syndicate carries the same obligations: condo fees and common charges, the contingency fund, the annual general meeting, the syndicate's insurance and a reconstruction-cost appraisal at least every five years.

  • Seasonal contracts: snow removal and landscaping, with several quotes on everything.
  • Work: calls for tenders, supervision and the maintenance log, so the building keeps its value.
  • Emergencies: water damage or a breakdown, the 24/7 line answers day and night, and every incident is documented.
  • Co-owners: clear communication and minutes sent on time, to keep conflicts from escalating.

Law 16: maintenance log, contingency fund study, certificate

Every syndicate in Québec needs a maintenance log and a contingency fund study by August 2028, and the syndicate's certificate, issued within 15 days of a request, already applies. These documents are prepared by an authorized independent professional — engineer, architect, chartered appraiser, professional technologist or CPA — not by your manager. We explain the obligations to the board, obtain several quotes, prepare the building's data, build the conclusions into the budget and keep the maintenance log up to date year after year, with no commission on the cost of the study.
