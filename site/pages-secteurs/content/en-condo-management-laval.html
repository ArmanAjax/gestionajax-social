Managing a condo in Laval

Laval is Québec's third-largest city. It was formed in 1965 by merging the fourteen municipalities of Île Jésus, between the Rivière des Prairies and the Rivière des Mille Îles, and its condo stock ranges from small six-unit buildings to large multi-building complexes. A syndicate's obligations are the same everywhere: condo fees and common charges, the contingency fund, the annual general meeting, the syndicate's insurance and a reconstruction-cost appraisal at least every five years.

  • Winter: snow removal contracts for entrances and parking, negotiated with several quotes.
  • Water damage: preventive maintenance, backwater valves and a 24/7 emergency line that answers at night.
  • Insurance: renewal, claims follow-up and the reconstruction-cost appraisal, all part of administration.
  • Budget: monthly financial statements and a contingency fund based on the study, to avoid special assessments.

Law 16: maintenance log, contingency fund study, certificate

Every syndicate in Québec needs a maintenance log and a contingency fund study by August 2028, and the syndicate's certificate, issued within 15 days of a request, already applies. These documents are prepared by an authorized independent professional — engineer, architect, chartered appraiser, professional technologist or CPA — not by your manager. We explain the obligations to the board, obtain several quotes, prepare the building's data, build the conclusions into the budget and keep the maintenance log up to date year after year, with no commission on the cost of the study.
